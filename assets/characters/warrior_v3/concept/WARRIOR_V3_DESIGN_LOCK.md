# Warrior_V3 — Design Lock v1

Status: CANONICAL CONTRACT FOR STAGE 1

Authority: `warrior_v3_master.png`. Where the master is visually ambiguous, this document chooses the simpler construction. No other view may introduce a new piece.

## Global proportions

- Height: 5.25 heads from boot sole to hair crown, tolerance ±0.1 head.
- Moderately enlarged head; compact athletic torso; shoulder width 2.05 head widths.
- Straight T-pose: upper arms horizontal, elbows extended, palms down, fingers readable.
- Hands 8% larger and boots 12% larger than neutral realistic anatomy.
- Legs slightly separated; both soles share one ground line.

## Hair

- One coherent dark royal-blue mass, approximately 14 large sculpted locks; no fine strands.
- Five dominant crown locks sweep upward and toward the character's right; four side/fringe locks frame the forehead; five rear locks close the back silhouette.
- Front volume rises about 0.55 head above the headband; rear volume projects about 0.28 head; lateral width is about 1.25 head widths.
- Crown height, rear projection and side width must remain volumetrically compatible in every view.

## Headband

- One continuous matte cream band, about 0.14 head high.
- Horizontal center sits just above the eyebrows and crosses behind the side hair.
- It wraps fully around the head at the same height.
- No visible knot, bow, long tail or side ornament.

## Scarf

- One thick red collar wrapped once around the neck, with consistent rounded volume.
- Exactly ONE short tail.
- Tail origin: posterior-left side of the character's neck.
- Tail path: falls down the character's left upper back, angled slightly outward.
- Tail end: single broad tapered end, never forked; ends at the lower edge of the left shoulder blade/upper back, never below the lower rib cage.
- The tail never changes side, never becomes a cape and never duplicates.

## Torso and shirt

- Base: one ivory short-sleeve shirt; one rolled cuff per arm, both identical.
- Over it: one sleeveless brown leather jerkin.
- Jerkin front: exactly two mirrored chest panels separated by one vertical center seam.
- One dark-brown shoulder yoke per side; no extra shoulder armor.
- Lower jerkin: exactly four broad skirt panels — front-left, front-right, back-left, back-right — all ending at the same upper-thigh height.
- Gold-brown piping follows only outer panel edges; visible stitch marks are material detail, not extra geometry.

## Belts and straps

- Exactly one horizontal dark-brown waist belt, centered at the natural waist, with one matte silver rectangular buckle at center front.
- Zero diagonal straps. The ambiguous sling from the original master is removed as a non-identity-critical detail.
- No chest or back straps; only the single horizontal waist belt remains.

## Pouches

- Zero pouches. They are removed from the canonical design.
- No bag, holster, satchel or belt attachment may be invented in another view.

## Bracers and hands

- Left and right bracers are identical mirrored pieces.
- Dark brown leather, from wrist to just below elbow, with three broad horizontal bands.
- Matching fingerless gloves; no metal plates or asymmetrical buckles.

## Pants

- One pair of plain charcoal trousers.
- Same loose-tapered cut front and back; no side panels, knee pads, stripes or hanging fabric.

## Boots

- Identical mirrored mid-calf brown boots.
- One wide folded cuff at top, two horizontal leather straps on shin, one instep strap.
- One matte silver toe cap per boot; no heel armor or additional plates.
- Same shaft height, sole thickness and toe silhouette in every view.

## Palette and materials

- Hair: dark royal blue with restrained medium-blue highlights.
- Headband/shirt: warm cream.
- Scarf: saturated red with wine-red shadow.
- Jerkin/boots/bracers: warm medium brown plus dark-brown structural bands.
- Pants: charcoal near-black. Metal: matte neutral silver. Skin: warm tan.
- Soft-toon, hand-painted color blocks, medium/high roughness, no plastic gloss.

## Explicit exclusions

- No sword, shield, armor plate suit, cape, second scarf tail, pouch, extra strap or back ornament.
- No change of clothing count, side, height, color or silhouette between views.
- No realistic 7–8-head anatomy, extreme chibi, voxel, toy or primitive construction.
